Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical timeline for website development?

Most projects are completed within 4-6 weeks, following our proven 5-phase development process. Complex e-commerce or custom functionality may require additional time. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

What ongoing support do you provide after launch?

We offer comprehensive maintenance packages including regular updates, security monitoring, performance optimization, and 24/7 emergency support. Our team remains available for content updates and technical assistance as needed.

How do you handle website security?

Security is paramount. We implement SSL certificates, regular backups, firewall protection, and continuous monitoring. All our websites include built-in security features and follow the latest security best practices.

What's included in your standard website package?

Our standard package includes custom design, responsive development, SEO optimization, content management system, contact forms, social media integration, and basic analytics setup. We also provide training for your team.

How do you ensure mobile responsiveness?

We follow a mobile-first design approach, testing on multiple devices and screen sizes. Our responsive framework ensures optimal viewing experience across all platforms, from smartphones to desktop computers.
